# XIV MIDI Studio

**Arrange like a producer with XIV MIDI Studio, a MIDI-focused digital audio workstation built for Final Fantasy XIV Bard Performance.**

XIV MIDI Studio brings Meowchestra’s MidiEditor quality-of-life features into a redesigned workspace inspired by Logic Pro X, Ableton Live, and Soundtrap.

Open a MIDI file, assign SoundFont instruments to your tracks, and arrange the piece in a multitrack timeline. Split, trim, move, or loop a phrase in the Arrangement, then edit its notes in the Piano Roll below. When you’re ready, export your arrangement as a standard MIDI file.

## Built for Bard arrangers

- **Arrange in regions.** Move a phrase, trim its edges, or drag its corner to repeat it.
- **Edit one region at a time.** The Piano Roll follows the selected region. Switch to **All Tracks** to see how the parts line up, just as you would in MidiEditor.
- **Choose which chord voices to keep.** Use the FFXIV chord tools to keep the highest note, the lowest note, or the inner voices.
- **Edit drum parts without guessing.** Turn on **GM Drum Map** to see General MIDI percussion names beside the Piano Roll keys. Find the kick, snare, or closed hi-hat by name before you edit its notes.
- **Audition your arrangement.** Choose SoundFont presets from the track headers and mute or solo tracks during playback.
- **Quantize by region.** Adjust timing, strength, and swing independently for each region. Whole-project quantization is planned.
